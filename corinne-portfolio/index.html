About

I'm a Master of Social Work student at The University of Texas at Austin, preparing for clinical practice with adolescents, adults, and older adults. In session, I draw on cognitive behavioral therapy, solution-focused brief therapy, and motivational interviewing.

My path to clinical work started in prevention. I studied global public health and social work at NYU, then spent several years in violence prevention research. I helped evaluate child sexual abuse prevention programs delivered to caregivers and schools across Pennsylvania, and I now support studies on child abuse prevention and youth with problematic sexual behaviors at the Center for Violence Prevention Research. Some of that work has been published in peer-reviewed journals.

I've also worked directly with young people: leading social and emotional learning groups at a youth center in London, teaching health workshops to high school students, and serving as a camp counselor. Research shows me what helps at the population level, and working with kids and teens reminds me that every person in the room has their own story. I want to bring both into my practice.
